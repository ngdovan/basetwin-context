BaseTwin vocabulary

Namespace https://w3id.org/basetwin/ns# (prefix bt:). JSON-LD context: https://w3id.org/basetwin/context/v1.jsonld. Within v1 terms are only added, never renamed or removed.

TermIRIMeaning
bt:kindhttps://w3id.org/basetwin/ns#kindNature of a value: observation, estimate, forecast or simulation. Always present.
bt:qualityhttps://w3id.org/basetwin/ns#qualityraw, provisional, validated or suspect.
bt:methodhttps://w3id.org/basetwin/ns#methodHow the value was produced, e.g. model:<name>@<version>.
bt:issuedAthttps://w3id.org/basetwin/ns#issuedAtWhen the value was issued (model outputs, forecasts); used for as-of reads.
bt:receivedAthttps://w3id.org/basetwin/ns#receivedAtWhen the value was received by the twin.
bt:validFromhttps://w3id.org/basetwin/ns#validFromStart of the validity interval.

bt:uncertaintyhttps://w3id.org/basetwin/ns#uncertaintyUncertainty of the value, e.g. {p10, p90, sigma}.
bt:spatialReshttps://w3id.org/basetwin/ns#spatialResTrue spatial resolution of the value, e.g. jis4 (JIS X 0410 mesh level) or point.
bt:temporalReshttps://w3id.org/basetwin/ns#temporalResTrue temporal resolution, ISO 8601 duration.
bt:provenancehttps://w3id.org/basetwin/ns#provenanceRelationship to the IngestRun or ModelRun that produced the value.
